# Safari Capture Minigame
A G1Rdeluxe's mod that reworks the safari zone catching mechanic into a timing based minigame.

*Disclaimer: I'm an artist, not a developer. All design choices and ideas present in this mod are my own, but the code itself was written by Claude, based from [harry4444's Fishing Mini Game](https://github.com/harryw4444/fishing_mini_game_gen1recomp) work.*

I always hated the original safari zone mechanics. It felt like pure luck and repetitive grind.
So, inspired by [harry4444's Fishing Mini Game mod](https://github.com/harryw4444/fishing_mini_game_gen1recomp), I decided to work on this mod. A timing based minigame, with scalable difficulty based on the pokemon you're trying to catch. Here's how it works:

When the encounter starts, you have 10 seconds to throw three safari balls.
Each time you correctly hit the safari ball on the pokemon inside the red square by pressing A, difficulty will increase. Hit three times in a row, and you capture the pokemon, but if you miss a ball, it'll run away.

The green bar at the bottom is your timer. If you take too long and your ten seconds end, the pokemon will automatically run away.

The base difficulty changes depending on the pokemon you're trying to catch, and how hard their capture is in the original. A Nidoran will be a cakewalk during all three throws, but a Kangaskhan will be significantly harder.

**Extras:**

Inside the mod's options you can find toggles to enable infinite time duration, so you can explore the safari zone with no rush, and infinite safari balls on your bag. This is completely optional, and should come disabled by default.

**How to install:**
- Download the latest .zip file on the [releases page](https://github.com/setogabes/safari-capture-minigame/releases)
- Open G1Rdeluxe's launcher and click on "Mods"
- Click on "Import Mod.zip", and select the one you just downloaded

*Reminder: This was made for Gen 1 games (RBY), and was not tested on later titles.*
